## 1. Getting Started

### Registration

1. Open the Sahovat website
2. Tap **Login** in the top navigation bar
3. Enter your Uzbek phone number in the format `+998 XX XXX XX XX`
4. Tap **Send OTP** -- you will receive a 6-digit code via SMS
5. Enter the OTP code on the verification screen
6. If you are a new user, you will be asked to complete your profile:
   - **Display name** (required)
   - **Date of birth** (optional)
   - **Gender** (optional)
   - **Preferred categories** -- select the types of campaigns you are interested in (medical, education, emergency, etc.)
   - **Language** -- Uzbek, Russian, or English
7. Tap **Complete Registration**

You are now logged in and can browse, donate, and create campaigns.

### Language

Sahovat supports three languages: **Uzbek** (uz), **Russian** (ru), and **English** (en). To switch languages, use the language switcher in the top navigation bar. Your preference is saved to your profile.

## 2. Browsing Campaigns

### Discovery Feed

The **home page** shows a curated feed of active campaigns. If you are logged in, the feed is personalized based on your interests and donation history.

### Browse All Campaigns

Navigate to **Campaigns** to see all active campaigns. You can:

- **Search** by title or description
- **Filter** by category (Medical, Education, Emergency, Community, Creative, Business)
- **Filter** by region (all 14 Uzbekistan regions)
- **Sort** by: newest, most funded, ending soon, or urgency

### Campaign Detail

Click on any campaign card to see its full details:

- **Progress bar** showing how much has been raised vs. the goal
- **Description** and story from the organizer
- **Supporting documents** (medical reports, official letters, etc.)
- **Donor comments** (from non-anonymous donors)
- **Days remaining** (if the campaign has an end date)
- **Share buttons** to spread the word

## 3. Making a Donation

### Standard Donation

1. On a campaign detail page, tap the **Donate** button
2. Select a preset amount (10K, 50K, 100K, 500K, 1M UZS) or enter a custom amount
3. Optionally:
   - Toggle **Anonymous** to hide your name from all users
   - Add a **comment** for the organizer
4. Choose your payment method:
   - **Saved card** (if you have one)
   - **PayMe redirect** (opens PayMe checkout page)
5. Confirm the donation and review the fee breakdown
6. Complete payment
7. After successful payment, you can download your **PDF receipt**

### High-Value Donations (Over 100,000 UZS)

For security, donations over 100,000 UZS require an additional OTP verification:

1. You will receive an SMS with a 6-digit code
2. Enter the code to confirm your donation
3. Then proceed with payment as usual

### Donation Receipts

After every successful donation, a PDF receipt is generated. You can:

- Download it immediately from the success screen
- Access it later from **My Donations** in the dashboard

## 4. Saving Payment Cards

Save your UzCard or Humo card for faster future donations.

1. Go to **Dashboard** > **Payment Methods**
2. Tap **Add Card**
3. Enter your 16-digit card number and expiry date (MMYY)
4. You will receive an OTP on the phone number linked to your card
5. Enter the OTP to verify and save the card
6. Your card is now saved for future donations

You can:
- **Set a default card** -- this card will be pre-selected when donating
- **Remove a card** -- deletes the tokenized card from PayMe and our system

Card numbers are never stored on our servers. We only store a secure token from PayMe.

## 5. Creating a Campaign

### Prerequisites

Before creating a campaign, you must:
- Have a **verified account** (upload an ID document from your profile)
- Have at least one **withdrawal account** set up (so you can receive funds)
- Set a **password** for your account (for security on sensitive actions)

### Campaign Creation Wizard

1. Go to **Dashboard** > **Create Campaign**
2. **Step 1 -- Basic Info:**
   - Campaign title
   - Category (Medical, Education, Emergency, etc.)
   - Fundraising goal (in UZS)
   - Region (optional)
   - End date (optional)
3. **Step 2 -- Story:**
   - Write a detailed description explaining your campaign
   - Why you need help, how the funds will be used
4. **Step 3 -- Cover Image:**
   - Upload a cover photo (JPEG, PNG, or WebP, max 5MB)
   - This is the first thing donors see
5. **Step 4 -- Documents:**
   - Upload supporting documents (medical reports, IDs, financial statements, etc.)
   - Up to 15 files. Mark sensitive documents as private (admin-only access)
6. **Step 5 -- Review & Submit:**
   - Review all details
   - Select your withdrawal account
   - Submit for admin review

After submission, your campaign enters **Pending Review** status. An admin will review your documents and either approve or request more information.

## 6. Managing Your Campaign

### My Campaigns

Go to **Dashboard** > **My Campaigns** to see all your campaigns with status badges:

- **Draft** -- not yet submitted
- **Pending Review** -- awaiting admin verification
- **Active** -- live and accepting donations
- **Paused** -- temporarily paused
- **Completed** -- reached goal or past end date
- **Frozen** -- frozen by admin (contact support)

### Organizer Dashboard

For each active campaign, the organizer dashboard shows:

- Total donations received
- Number of donors
- Available balance (total donated minus withdrawn minus pending withdrawals)
- Withdrawal history
- Progress toward the goal

## 7. Requesting Withdrawals

### Setting Up Withdrawal Accounts

1. Go to **Dashboard** > **Withdrawal Accounts**
2. Tap **Add Account**
3. Enter:
   - **Provider:** PayMe, UzCard, or Humo
   - **Card number:** 16-digit card number
   - **Cardholder name:** must match your legal name (admin will verify)
4. Set one account as **primary**

### Making a Withdrawal Request

1. Go to your campaign's **Manage** page
2. Tap **Request Withdrawal**
3. Select the campaign to withdraw from
4. Enter the amount (up to your available balance)
5. Select your withdrawal account
6. Review the fee breakdown (platform fee is deducted)
7. Submit the request

### Withdrawal Process

1. Your request enters **Pending** status
2. An admin reviews the request, comparing your verified legal name with the cardholder name on your withdrawal account
3. If approved, the admin processes the manual transfer and marks it as **Completed** with a transaction reference
4. You can track the status in **Dashboard** > **Withdrawals**

## 8. Recurring Donations

Set up automatic donations to your favorite campaigns or categories.

### Setting Up

1. When donating, choose the **Recurring** option
2. Or go to **Dashboard** > **Recurring Donations** > **Create New**
3. Choose:
   - A specific **campaign** or a **category** (e.g., all Medical campaigns)
   - **Amount** per charge
   - **Frequency:** weekly or monthly
4. Confirm setup

### Managing Recurring Donations

From **Dashboard** > **Recurring Donations** you can:

- **Pause** a subscription (skip upcoming charges)
- **Resume** a paused subscription
- **Cancel** a subscription permanently
- View your **impact stats:** total donated, campaigns supported, donation streak

### Failure Handling

If a recurring charge fails (insufficient funds, expired card, etc.):

1. The system retries automatically
2. After 3 failed attempts, the subscription is automatically paused
3. You can resume it after resolving the payment issue

## 9. Profile Management

### Editing Your Profile

Go to **Dashboard** > **Profile** to update:

- Display name
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Preferred categories (affects your personalized feed)
- Language preference (Uzbek, Russian, English)

### Identity Verification

To create campaigns, you need to verify your identity:

1. Go to **Profile** > **Verification**
2. Upload a photo of your passport or national ID
3. Our system will process your document
4. An admin will review and approve your verification

Once verified, you can create campaigns and request withdrawals.

## 10. FAQ

**Q: Is my donation information private?**
A: If you select "Anonymous," your name is hidden from everyone -- the organizer, other donors, and even admins cannot see your identity on that donation. Your donation is still recorded internally for legal compliance.

**Q: What are the platform fees?**
A: The platform charges a small percentage fee (configurable by admin, typically 1%) on donations. The fee is shown before you confirm your donation.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Contact the platform administrator. Refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis.

**Q: What payment methods are supported?**
A: Currently PayMe (UzCard and Humo cards). Additional providers may be added in the future.

**Q: How long do withdrawals take?**
A: Withdrawals are manually reviewed by administrators. Typical processing time is 1-3 business days after approval.

**Q: Is my data stored securely?**
A: Yes. Card numbers are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. All data is stored on servers in Uzbekistan in compliance with data localization laws.

**Q: Can I use Sahovat on mobile?**
A: Sahovat is a responsive web application that works on all screen sizes. There is no separate mobile app -- just visit the website from your phone's browser.
